\begin{abstract}
Catalogues of resistance-conferring mutations of the \textit{Mycobacterium tuberculosis}
complex are published per variant, in gene-based HGVS notation, so a pipeline comparing
genomes with one must first express each entry as a genomic coordinate string. The
coordinate file of the second edition of the World Health Organization catalogue gives
144\,964 lines for 41\,358 variants, 3.5 per variant, and 60.7\,\% of the variants receive
more than one; 97\,787 of those lines are multi-nucleotide blocks. Our ingestion kept one
representation per variant, preferring the single-base form, and so discarded 83\,608 of
the 120\,917 representations it could have emitted for the variants it shares with the file
(69.1\,\%), 77\,073 of them blocks. We measure what that rule costs on a matrix of 50\,782
strains whose variant calls keep blocks. Under the ingested catalogue, decomposing each
observed block into single-base components raises baseline sensitivity by 57.4 points for
moxifloxacin (95\,\% interval 29.3 to 63.3) and 50.0 for levofloxacin (24.1 to 57.4); when
the blocks the source already provides are emitted, the same measurement gives 13.2 (7.9 to
15.6) and 11.7 (6.3 to 13.3), so 77.0\,\% (66.2 to 79.5) and 76.5\,\% (67.7 to 79.7) of the
apparent gap follows from the ingestion rule. A pipeline matching both ways reaches the same
sensitivity either way (0.841 and 0.799), so the rule costs only a pipeline that matches
exactly. The residual gap is carried by three five-base haplotypes of \textit{gyrA} codon 94
on a Ser95Thr background, for which the file gives no entry although it gives one for five
of the eight variants of that codon, twice in that same five-base form. Emitting every representation triples the catalogue, changes final
sensitivity by less than one point elsewhere, and gives nineteen pairs of a coordinate
string and a medicine two contradictory verdicts. Three published implementations match
above the coordinate level, each by a different strategy read here in its source code.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Genome-based prediction of antitubercular drug resistance relies on catalogues that map
genetic variants to a resistance verdict. The second edition of the World Health
Organization catalogue \cite{who2023} grades the variants of the complex for 15 medicines,
and rule-based tools such as TB-Profiler \cite{phelan2019,verboven2022}, Mykrobe
\cite{hunt2019} and the piezo grammar used by the CRyPTIC consortium apply catalogues of
this kind to sequenced isolates. A catalogue entry is written at the level of the variant,
as a gene name and a change in HGVS notation, and the catalogue of the second edition is
distributed together with a file that gives the genomic coordinates of each entry.

When a pipeline builds a strain by variant matrix, which is what a
machine-learning predictor, a bacterial genome-wide association study and a deterministic
rule engine all consume, the natural key is a coordinate string: a chromosome, a position,
a reference allele and an alternative allele, in the form used by the SPDI data model
\cite{holmes2020}. The same change in a genome can be written as several such strings,
because a sequence variant has no canonical coordinate form unless one is imposed
\cite{tan2015}: two adjacent substitutions inside a codon can be written as two
single-base changes or as one block of equal length, and an insertion or a deletion in a
repeated context can be left-aligned or right-aligned. The coordinate file of the
catalogue reflects this, and gives several representations for most of its entries. An
ingestion that keeps one of them per variant therefore makes a choice, and the choice is
silent downstream: an isolate whose call does not use the representation that was kept is
simply counted as carrying no determinant, and is predicted susceptible.

Neither the phenomenon nor its remedies are new. Verboven et al. \cite{verboven2022} state
the difficulty of format for the first edition of the catalogue, noting that the way
variants are listed makes a classification hard to look up when the sequencing data have
been processed by another pipeline, especially for insertions and deletions. Outside tuberculosis the same mechanism has
been measured on the calling side. Srinivasan et al. \cite{srinivasan2021} report 12\,141
misannotated multi-nucleotide variants (MNVs) in 10\,383 variant files of The Cancer
Genome Atlas, trace them to callers that emit adjacent substitutions separately, so that
the amino acid deduced from each differs from the one the combined change produces, and
recommend merging adjacent calls into MNVs as a best practice of sequencing pipelines.
Wang et al. \cite{wang2020} identify 1\,792\,248 MNVs in the exomes and genomes of the
Genome Aggregation Database, 18\,756 of them with a combined effect on the protein
sequence that neither constituent predicts, and conclude for haplotype-aware annotation.
Repun \cite{repun2024} unifies variant representation across sequencing platforms,
precisely because a representation that differs between platforms defeats comparison by
equality. What none of these works gives is the cost of the choice in the currency of the
application that makes it: points of sensitivity, medicine by medicine, on a cohort of
phenotyped isolates.

A reader may hold that a chain which keeps one representation
per variant has a defect of its own making, that correcting it is ordinary maintenance,
and that a pipeline matching above the coordinate level never had anything to gain, a
reading that the results below support, since the complete baseline reaches the same
sensitivity under both ingestions. Part of that is conceded here: the larger
share of the gap reported below follows from a rule written in our own ingestion code, and
we name no chain outside this project that applies the same rule. Three considerations
nevertheless make the measurement worth reporting. The residual that remains once every
representation of the source has been emitted is carried by calls that combine a graded
determinant with an adjacent variant, and the coordinate file enumerates such combined
haplotypes for five of the eight variants of the codon concerned and not for the three that
carry that residual, twice in exactly the form the three missing lines would take, so that
residual records an enumeration that is incomplete, and uneven in form, and not one that is
impossible (Section~\ref{sec:residuel}). Nothing establishes that the class of exposed chains is
empty, although no chain of the complex is named here: in cancer genomics, where the
mechanism had been diagnosed and a best practice recommended, 20 of the 178 published
studies whose calls on seven commonly mutated genes were examined in a public portal were
still missing MNVs \cite{srinivasan2021}, and a systematic review of tuberculosis
prediction tools published in 2026 names the variant-calling pipeline among the unmeasured
covariates of their performance heterogeneity \cite{peng2026}, which is a covariate
adjacent to the ingestion rule rather than that rule itself. Finally, the construction at
stake is the strain by variant matrix that a statistical predictor such as GenTB
\cite{groschel2021} or a bacterial genome-wide association study consumes, rather than the
diagnostic pipeline; a coordinate string is the straightforward key for such a matrix, and
none of the three implementations read in Section~\ref{sec:outils} intervenes in its
construction. What the published chains of that kind use as a key is not established here.

The subject of these measurements is an ingestion chain and not the catalogue, which supplies
the representations that the chain discards, and the claim of novelty is restricted
accordingly. We do not claim that the phenomenon, its mechanism or its remedies are
undescribed, since the works cited above describe them; we report that the search of
Section~\ref{sec:anterieur} returned no measurement of what the choice costs a resistance
prediction.

\section{Materials and methods}

\subsection{Catalogue and its coordinate source}
The consolidated catalogue used here is a long table with one row per assertion of the
form variant, medicine, source and verdict, built by merging the master file of the WHO
catalogue \cite{who2023}, the reference database of TB-Profiler \cite{phelan2019} and an
empirical signal derived from the CRyPTIC compendium \cite{cryptic2022}. It holds 64\,052
rows over 18 medicines. The three sources are stacked rather than arbitrated: each contributes
its own assertions, so one variant can carry assertions of several sources, and each row keeps
the grade of its own source, normalised to three values. Of the five confidence grades of the
WHO catalogue, grades 1 and 2 become an association with resistance, grade 3 uncertain, and
grades 4 and 5 an absence of association. HGVS notation is resolved to the coordinate convention of the
project with the official WHO genomic-coordinates file, applying the one-base offset
between the VCF convention of that file and the zero-based convention of the project, a
correspondence validated against the pangenome. The resolver returns every representation
of a variant whose reference and alternative alleles are at most 24 bases long; the
3\,876 lines of the file (2.7\,\%) that exceed this length are never emitted.

\subsection{Two ingestions of the same source}
Two catalogues are built from the same source files and differ by one rule. In the
ingested catalogue, written C0 below, the builder collects the representations of a
variant, keeps those whose reference and alternative alleles are single bases, and emits
the block representations only when no single-base form exists. In the second catalogue,
written C1, every representation within the length limit is emitted. C0 is the catalogue
that the project has been using; re-running the chain with its defaults reproduces it as a
set of rows exactly (64\,052 rows, none in excess and none missing), and C0 is contained
in C1.

The preference for the single-base form is written twice in the chain. The index of the
HGVS resolver keeps one single-base representation per variant and exposes it as the
resolution of that variant, and the catalogue builder applies the same preference to the
full table of representations. The loss counted below is produced by the builder, since it
reads the full table rather than the reduced index.

\subsection{Genotypes, phenotypes and the matrix that keeps raw calls}
Binary susceptibility phenotypes measured in the laboratory were consolidated from the
CRyPTIC reuse table \cite{cryptic2022}, from the expanded CRyPTIC release
\cite{cryptic2026dataset} which brings twelve drug-susceptibility testing sources, from the
NCBI BioSample antibiogram tag read through the TB-Annotator research database
\cite{senelle2023}, from a published association panel \cite{coll2018}, from the PATRIC
antimicrobial-resistance table, whose labels for moxifloxacin, amikacin and capreomycin
come almost entirely from one Russian surveillance collection \cite{casali2014}, and from
a few literature entries. When a strain and a
medicine carry labels from several sources, the consolidated table keeps the source that
ranks first in an order fixed beforehand, a property of the source and never of the
genotype. One source of the project, a set of curated dated isolates, is excluded
throughout, because its labels are computed from the genotype and would make any
sensitivity circular; a pair that loses its label this way falls back on the next source.

Variant profiles were read from two generations of the genotyping pipeline that keep the
blocks emitted by the variant caller: a precomputed pangenome (41\,484 strains) and the
per-strain genotype files of the research database \cite{senelle2023} (9\,298 strains), which
agree where they overlap. In that pipeline, reads are aligned to H37Rv with BWA-MEM and
variants are called by snippy, which runs freebayes in diploid-free mode and keeps the calls
homozygous for the alternative allele above a quality and a depth threshold; Supplementary
Note~S3 gives the versions, the options and the thresholds. That freebayes assembles adjacent
substitutions into one haplotype call is why blocks are present in these data at all: the
block question is a property of the caller and of its options, not of the genomes. The matrix built from them holds 50\,782 strains, 41\,551 of
them carrying at least one measured phenotype among the eleven medicines of
Table~\ref{tab:medicaments}. Every catalogued determinant is kept as a column whatever its
frequency. The question asked here cannot be posed on the other matrices of the project:
the matrix used for the learned predictors brings all three genotype generations to one
representation, with every block split into single-base variants, and the third generation,
read from the tblearn server, does not keep blocks at all. Lineage
assignments in the Coll system were retrieved for the resampling described below.

The labels are heterogeneous, and the absolute sensitivities reported here are
compositions of their sources: the 121\,620 labelled pairs (51.1\,\% of 237\,887) that come
from the expanded CRyPTIC release rest on eleven of its twelve testing sources, which use
different methods; several of the cohorts are reported by their authors to have served in
building earlier catalogues, the first edition of the WHO catalogue among them, and the
CRyPTIC-v1.0 cohort that the release notes \cite{cryptic2026dataset} describe as handed over for that purpose supplies
92\,174 pairs (38.7\,\%); and one surveillance study (BioProject PRJNA1237251) supplies 24.3,
23.9 and 22.0\,\% of the susceptible isolates of isoniazid, rifampicin and ethambutol and under
6\,\% of the resistant ones. These absolute values are not the
object of this report. Every comparison below holds the strains, the labels and the matrix
fixed and varies only the ingestion of the catalogue, so that the composition of the
labels is common to the two arms and cancels in the difference.

\subsection{Deterministic baseline and measurement by removal}
The deterministic baseline labels a strain resistant to a medicine when it carries at least
one determinant that the catalogue grades as associated with resistance for that medicine,
and only those: the uncertain rows and the rows of absence of association are never read, so
a coordinate string carrying contradictory verdicts from two sources is read as associated
with resistance. Complete matching proceeds in three ways: single-base variants are matched
exactly, an observed multi-nucleotide block is decomposed into its single-base components
which are then matched, and insertions and deletions are matched exactly against every
catalogued representation. Two further steps bear not on the matching but on which
determinants exist to be matched: codon propagation, which emits for a protein-level variant
every nucleotide representation of the target amino acid rather than one, and the inclusion of
rare determinants, which keeps a catalogued determinant as a column whatever its frequency.
The four steps measured below are therefore the block step, the indel step, codon propagation
and the rare-determinant step. Sensitivity and specificity are given per medicine on the
measured phenotypes, with Clopper--Pearson 95\,\% intervals (Supplementary Table~S3).

The corrections of the chain were introduced one after another and the states before them
were not archived, so the contribution of each is measured by removal: the sensitivity of
the complete baseline minus the sensitivity of the baseline deprived of one of the four steps
just defined, the others remaining active; deprived of the rare-determinant step, the baseline
drops the determinants present in fewer than 20 strains, as the frequency filter of a feature
matrix does. Intervals on a difference come from 2\,000 resamplings of the sub-lineage codes
as clusters, the same draws serving the complete and the deprived baseline, and serving both
catalogues, which makes the ratio of two differences a paired quantity. The two catalogues are evaluated on the same matrix, the one
rebuilt with C1, because a matrix built with C0 would lack the rare block columns and the
comparison would then confound two effects.

Two controls frame the measurement. The chain re-run with its defaults reproduces the
ingested catalogue, as stated above. The matrix of the previous run of this project,
evaluated with C0, reproduces the sensitivities published from it to the third decimal for
the fluoroquinolones; the only difference is at most 0.14 point on rifampicin, because
that earlier audit did not count the exact match of a block none of whose components is a
catalogued determinant, whereas the measurement reported here counts an exact match or a
decomposed one.

\subsection{Reading of three published implementations}
The matching strategies described in Section~\ref{sec:outils}, those of TB-Profiler,
Mykrobe \cite{hunt2019} and piezo \cite{piezo}, the grammar being applied to genomes by
gnomonicus \cite{gnomonicus}, were read in the public source code of the tools, in the
state of their repositories on 16 November 2026; Supplementary Note~S2 lists the commits
and files. None of the three was run on the data of this study, so that section describes
how each matches and not how each performs.

\section{Results}

\subsection{The source gives several representations per variant, the ingestion keeps one}
The coordinate file of the catalogue holds 144\,964 lines with a readable variant,
position, reference allele and alternative allele, for 41\,358 distinct variants, that is
3.5 lines per variant. More than one representation is given for 25\,118 variants
(60.7\,\%), and for 16\,184 of the 18\,732 variants written at the protein level
(86.4\,\%). By nature, 32\,155 lines are single-base changes, 97\,787 are blocks whose
reference and alternative alleles have the same length above one, 11\,146 are insertions
or deletions, and 3\,876 exceed the length limit of the resolver, which is a fourth class of
line and not a fourth nature. Indexed by variant rather than by coordinate, the file supplies
the alternative representations itself: for \textit{embB} Met306Ile it gives the three
alternative bases at one position together with a block of ten bases; for \textit{gyrA}
Asp94Gly it gives five representations, among them the four-base block
\texttt{ACAG}$\rightarrow$\texttt{GCAC}.

The ingestion keeps one representation per variant whenever a single-base form exists. On
the 30\,534 variants that the consolidated catalogue shares with the coordinate file, the
resolver can emit 120\,917 representations and the catalogue retains 37\,309: 83\,608 are
discarded, 69.1\,\%, of which 77\,073 are blocks, and 19\,154 variants (62.7\,\%) lose at
least one representation. The ingested catalogue consequently holds 52\,041 rows whose
coordinate string is a single-base change and 772 whose string is a block, over 32\,596 and
543 distinct strings respectively; with the 4\,642 distinct strings of its insertion and
deletion rows, these make up the 37\,781 distinct strings of the catalogue.

\subsection{Three quarters of the fluoroquinolone block gap follows from the ingestion rule}
Under the ingested catalogue,
removing the decomposition of observed blocks lowers moxifloxacin sensitivity from 0.841 to
0.267 and levofloxacin sensitivity from 0.799 to 0.300, differences of 57.4 and 50.0
points (Table~\ref{tab:fq}). Under the catalogue that emits the blocks of the source, the same removal costs
13.2 and 11.7 points, since matching exactly now recovers most of the carriers: 77.0\,\% of
the difference measured for moxifloxacin and 76.5\,\% of the difference measured for
levofloxacin follow from the rule applied at ingestion rather than from the written form of
the calls.

These intervals are wide and asymmetric, the lower bound of 29.3 points being half of the 57.4
estimated for moxifloxacin, which is the content of the resampling and not noise: the quantity
depends on the frequency of one sub-lineage marker, Ser95Thr, and the draws resample
sub-lineages. The ratio is far better determined than either difference, because both follow
that frequency together and the two arms share the draws; propagated through the same 2\,000
paired draws it is 77.0\,\% (66.2 to 79.5) and 76.5\,\% (67.7 to 79.7). The cohort fixes how
much of the gap the rule accounts for, not how large the gap is.

The sensitivity of the complete baseline is identical under the two ingestions, 0.841 for
moxifloxacin and 0.799 for levofloxacin, with identical specificities, so emitting the
blocks benefits a pipeline that matches exactly and adds nothing to a pipeline that
decomposes. The specificity cost of the decomposition falls with it
(Table~\ref{tab:fq}), because the strains
that the decomposition newly flags are, for the most part, already flagged by the exact
match of their block. Outside the fluoroquinolones the contribution of the decomposition
is at most 0.99 point under the ingested catalogue, for isoniazid, and disappears when the
blocks are emitted (Supplementary Table~S2).

\subsection{The residual gap is three haplotypes that the coordinate file does not list}
\label{sec:residuel}
Under the catalogue that emits every representation, exactly three columns of the matrix
still carry a catalogued determinant only through decomposition, and they account for the
residual 13.2 and 11.7 points. All three are five-base blocks at \textit{gyrA} codon 94 on
a Ser95Thr background: \texttt{GACAG}$\rightarrow$\texttt{AACAC} (Asp94Asn, 433 strains),
\texttt{GACAG}$\rightarrow$\texttt{TACAC} (Asp94Tyr, 221 strains) and
\texttt{GACAG}$\rightarrow$\texttt{CACAC} (Asp94His, 86 strains), 740 strains in all. Among
the strains they carry that have a moxifloxacin label, 239, 111 and 44 are resistant and 86
are susceptible, which is 13.5 points of the 2\,916 resistant strains for a measured
difference of 13.2 points; for levofloxacin, 172, 70 and 41 are resistant and 33 are
susceptible, 12.0 points of the 2\,358 resistant strains for a measured difference of 11.7
points. The accounting is therefore closed: the residual gap is these three haplotypes and
nothing else. The carrier share slightly exceeds the measured difference, by 0.3 point for
each of the two medicines, because a minority of these strains also carries another
catalogued determinant for the medicine and is recovered without the decomposition.

The coordinate file gives, for these three variants, the single-base change and the
three-base codon changes \texttt{GAC}$\rightarrow$\texttt{AAT},
\texttt{GAC}$\rightarrow$\texttt{TAT} and \texttt{GAC}$\rightarrow$\texttt{CAT}, and no
five-base form. The caller emits five bases because the adjacent Ser95Thr substitution is
part of the same block, and Ser95Thr is frequent in this cohort, being a marker of a
sub-lineage. No representation of Asp94Asn taken alone equals a block that also carries
Ser95Thr, so exact matching recovers these strains only from an entry that combines the two
substitutions. The file does carry such an entry for five of the eight variants of this codon,
and its enumeration is not merely incomplete: it is uneven in form from one amino acid to the
next at the same codon. For Asp94Gly, Asp94Val and Asp94Ala it gives a four-base block at
position 7582, which is the combined haplotype with Ser95Thr and agrees border for border
with the string the caller emits for those variants; Asp94Ala receives two, one with Ser95Thr
and one with Ser95Ala. For Asp94Phe and Asp94Leu it gives a five-base block at position 7581,
\texttt{GACAG}$\rightarrow$\texttt{TTCAC} and \texttt{GACAG}$\rightarrow$\texttt{CTCAC}, at
the position and in the form of the three strings above. What is missing for Asp94Asn,
Asp94Tyr and Asp94His is therefore not a notation able to express the haplotype, nor a line of
an unprecedented kind, but three lines of a form the file already carries twice at that very
position for other amino acids of the same codon: an identity, not an analogy.

other than the block decomposition under the two ingestions. Codon propagation is unchanged to
the second decimal everywhere, as expected: the single-base alternatives of a codon are
already emitted by the ingested catalogue, and the blocks add nothing to them. The
contributions of indel matching and of the rare-determinant step rise slightly, by 0.66 point
at most, because the file supplies alternative normalisations of an insertion or a deletion

full emission accordingly changes only the final sensitivities that depend on indels, those of
pyrazinamide, ethionamide, capreomycin, isoniazid and streptomycin, and by less than one

\subsection{What emitting every representation costs}\label{sec:emission}
The catalogue that emits every representation holds 204\,522 rows against 64\,052, that is
3.19 times as many, and contains the ingested catalogue. The 140\,470 added rows are
129\,459 blocks and 11\,011 insertions or deletions, almost all of them from the WHO source,
and they carry 99\,103 verdicts of uncertain association, 23\,540 of association with
resistance and 17\,827 of absence of association. The number of distinct coordinate strings
rises from 37\,781 to 115\,097, and the number of distinct strings carrying at least one
association with resistance from 4\,242 to 23\,958, of which 14\,670 are blocks. Those
14\,670 strings are the combinatorial space of the codons of the catalogued amino-acid
changes rather than a list of observed haplotypes, and the cohort bears this out: over the
eleven medicines, the number of matrix columns carrying a catalogued resistance determinant
rises only from 1\,389 to 1\,565, so the overwhelming majority of the added strings is
carried by no strain of the 50\,782.

The emission also creates contradictions that the ingested catalogue did not have. Nineteen
pairs of a coordinate string and a medicine receive both a verdict of association with
resistance and a verdict of absence of association, against none before (Supplementary
Table~S1). The clearest case is the four-base block of \textit{gyrA} codon 94: the same string
\texttt{ACAG}$\rightarrow$\texttt{GCAC} is a representation of Asp94Gly, graded as associated
with resistance, and a representation of Ser95Thr, graded as not associated, for both
fluoroquinolones. As stated in the methods, the matching reads only the assertions of
association with resistance, so the resistant verdict prevails by default; the figures above
are unaffected, and an arbitration would have to be specified if a catalogue of this form were
adopted.

\subsection{Three strategies that avoid matching at the coordinate level}\label{sec:outils}
None of the three published implementations examined here depends on a coordinate string
alone, and each avoids that dependence differently.

TB-Profiler, through the \texttt{pathogen-profiler} library, matches on several keys at
once. The annotation of a consequence collects, by union into one set, the entries found
under the keys (gene, nucleotide change in HGVS), (gene, protein change), (gene,
sequence-level HGVS string) and (gene, each sequence-ontology term of the consequence), and
the entries whose key declares a range of positions covering the consequence. The coordinate string is one key
among five families, and a protein-level entry is reached whatever the codon. The same
pipeline also performs the operation inverse to decomposition before annotating: in the
command that annotates a variant file, a step groups the calls by exon and codon position,
reconstructs the haplotype of the codon, and emits a single multi-nucleotide variant which is
then annotated at the protein level. Four conditions bound that step, each read in the code
and listed in Supplementary Note~S2: it groups only single-base calls inside a coding exon;
it counts haplotypes in the alignment only when a suitable alignment file is supplied, and
otherwise synthesises them from the depth fields of the variant file; one of the callers it
accepts removes that counting; and the whole step is skipped when the option that splits
insertions and deletions is turned off. Protein-level annotation remains the fallback in each
of these cases.

Mykrobe enumerates codons when it builds its probes. Its gene model inverts the bacterial
codon table, from amino acid to the list of its codons, and names one probe for the reference
codon of the genome against each codon of the target amino acid that the reference does not
share (Supplementary Note~S2). The enumeration is one-sided, running from the single reference
codon to every codon of the alternative amino acid; an amino-acid change is still captured
whichever codon realises it, and the probe being a whole codon, the block question does not
arise either. This behaviour has been published since 2019 \cite{hunt2019}.

The piezo grammar, which gnomonicus applies to genomes, is gene-centred. A rule is written
as a gene name, the character \texttt{@} and a mutation, and the prediction function splits
the pair on that character; the position is an amino-acid number for a coding sequence and
a nucleotide number for a ribosomal RNA gene or a promoter. No coordinate string appears in
a rule.

The authors of piezo and gnomonicus have since characterised that pair on 2\,663 isolates
under their own implementation of the second edition, and their comparison with TB-Profiler
finds the two tools comparable although their design choices differ \cite{westhead2025}. That
measurement compares two implementations at a fixed representation; it does not vary the
representation, which is the quantity measured here. A fourth published strategy dissolves the
question rather than answering it at the matching step, by encoding the haplotypes of
resistance and of susceptibility in a reference graph, where a haplotype is a path and not a
string compared by equality \cite{drprg2023}; such a graph is still built from a catalogue
distributed per variant, so the enumeration of Section~\ref{sec:residuel} bounds what any of
these strategies can find in the source.

\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Relation to previous work}\label{sec:anterieur}
The ingestion written C1 here, which emits every representation of a variant, is the
published design choice of Verboven et al. \cite{verboven2022} for insertions and
deletions, made on the previous edition of the catalogue. The restriction to insertions and
deletions matters, because the two works do not bear on the same class of variants: the
ambiguity they resolved by listing every possibility is the left or right alignment of one
indel in a repeated context, whereas 77\,073 of the 83\,608 representations dropped here
(92.2\,\%) are blocks of equal reference and alternative length, indels accounting for
11\,146 of the 144\,964 lines of the coordinate file. Their conversion reached 17\,061 of the
17\,356 variants of that edition, 98.3\,\%, a figure that counts variants a conversion can
express and carries no consequence for a prediction. Their 254 entries that group several
variants into a single indel are the nearest counterpart, on the catalogue side, of the
residual of Section~\ref{sec:residuel}, since HGVS notation does not admit several variants in
one entry unless they share a codon. The counterpart is nevertheless loose: their grouped
entries are present in the catalogue and cannot be written in the notation, whereas the three
haplotypes described here are absent
from the coordinate file although the notation expresses them, as the five-base entries given
for Asp94Phe and Asp94Leu show.

The two remedies applied here have both been published: emitting every representation
\cite{verboven2022} and treating the codon rather than the base as the unit of comparison
\cite{srinivasan2021,wang2020}. We searched for a published measurement of what the choice
between them costs a resistance prediction, in November 2026, in a local mirror of the
PubMed tuberculosis corpus, in the full text indexed by Europe PMC and on the web.
Supplementary Note~S1 gives the protocol: the measured state of the mirror, the seven queries
verbatim, the number of records each returned, the criterion of inclusion, and the candidates
whose abstract was read in full with the reason each was set aside. The queries returned 75
records, 51 of them on the one axis that bears directly on prediction tools, and none of the
75 measures the effect of a representation choice in points of sensitivity. A search that
returns nothing does not establish that nothing exists, so the claim is the narrow one: we
have found no such measurement.

\subsection{What follows for a chain that builds a matrix}
The gap measured here is a property of the ingestion chain. The cost is unevenly distributed: it is
large for the fluoroquinolones, where the resistance-determining codon of \textit{gyrA} is
adjacent to a frequent sub-lineage marker and is therefore called as a block, and it is
negligible for the medicines whose determinants are isolated substitutions or insertions and
deletions. This unevenness is what makes the defect hard to notice.

Two conclusions follow for anyone building a strain by variant matrix from a catalogue
published in HGVS. The first is that the matching procedure and the ingestion rule have to
be designed together, since the same sensitivity can be reached from either end. The second is that emitting every representation of the file as it stands is not
sufficient on its own. The residual thirteen points of moxifloxacin are carried by three
haplotypes that the coordinate file does not list, and listing them would mean enumerating
the combinations of a graded determinant with the variants called next to it, which this
file does for five of the eight variants of the codon and not for the three that carry the
residual. A source that grades one variant at a time can carry such combinations, as those
five entries show, but
carrying them all means paying on the catalogue side the combinatorial price measured in
Section~\ref{sec:emission}, and the set to enumerate depends on which neighbours a given
cohort actually calls. A step that decomposes the observed call, or a matching performed
above the coordinate level, settles that class without enumerating it, and it is precisely
the class that grows with the frequency of the neighbouring marker.

This report says nothing about the part of the unexplained resistance that is not a matter of
representation. That part has been partitioned elsewhere: on 509 isolates tested against nine
medicines, Hong et al. \cite{hong2026} attribute most of the discordance between the
genotypic and the phenotypic verdict to errors of the phenotypic test, to hetero-resistance
and to determinants recovered by another tool, and conclude that unknown mechanisms of
resistance are not its main cause.

\section{Limitations}

The measurement rests on one cohort, one caller and one catalogue. The matrix is the one that keeps the blocks emitted by the
variant caller, 50\,782 strains out of the 85\,509 that the project genotypes.

The residual gap was characterised at one codon. Three haplotypes of \textit{gyrA} codon 94
account for it on this cohort, but the blocks of \textit{gyrB} and of the other genes were
not examined one by one, and a cohort with different sub-lineage frequencies would give a
different residue. The 3\,876 coordinate lines longer than 24 bases are never emitted by the
resolver, and their effect was not measured.

The strategies of the three published tools were read in their source code, in one state of
their repositories, and none of the tools was executed here; no claim is made about their
sensitivity, nor about whether they recover the three haplotypes. Whether a learned
predictor trained on a matrix that keeps raw blocks loses the same sensitivity as the
deterministic baseline has not been measured. Finally, a third edition of the reference
catalogue was published in November 2026 and has not been ingested here, so the counts of
representations reported above describe the second edition.
